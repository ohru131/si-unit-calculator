## Narration

Speak in US English with a clear, confident, warm product-demo delivery at a steady conversational pace. Keep the tone practical and precise, with short pauses between sections:

> Unit mistakes slow down learning, engineering, and everyday problem solving. Unit Calculator Pro makes mixed-unit calculations feel reliable. Start with an expression like five centimeters plus one millimeter. Before calculating, the app normalizes every value to SI base units. Then it checks the dimensions, calculates the result, and lets you choose a compatible display unit. That means you can focus on the problem, not on manual conversion.
>
> The same approach works for derived quantities. Divide distance by time to calculate speed. Divide force by area for pressure. Combine energy and time for power. Built-in examples make each workflow easy to try, whether you are studying, prototyping, or checking a measurement on the job.
>
> For repeated work, save constants such as width and height, then reuse them in later expressions. Calculation history stays on your device and can be restored with one tap.
>
> Unit Calculator Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads, adds CSV export for documentation, and unlocks a personal unit set for the units you use most. The core calculator remains available to everyone. Pro is for people who want to make technical work repeatable.
>
> Unit Calculator Pro. Calculate with confidence, in any compatible unit.
